Your movie or music is on the Mac. The remote is in your hand.

Kinrox Remote controls Kinrox, a free, open-source video and music player for Mac, from your iPhone. Pause, skip and turn the volume down without leaving the couch, or right from the Lock Screen.

MOVIES
• Play and pause, jump back or ahead by 10 or 30 seconds, or scrub to any moment.
• Set the volume and toggle fullscreen on your Mac.
• Move to the previous or next video in the folder, or open a recent file.
• Start the Matrix screensaver when you take a break.

MUSIC
• Play, pause, skip and set the volume of the music playing on your Mac.
• Add the current track to favorites and manage the queue: reorder or remove tracks.
• Listen to similar: keep the music going with tracks like the one playing, and hide tracks or artists you are not interested in.

LOCK SCREEN
• While something plays, controls appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: pause, skip and volume without unlocking your iPhone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works over your local network with an encrypted connection, paired with a six-digit code.
• No account, cloud service, advertising or analytics.
• A demo lets you try the controls before connecting a Mac.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Kinrox for Mac, free at https://kinrox.app
• An iPhone with iOS 17 or later on the same local network as the Mac.

Music mode works with the music library you connect in Kinrox for Mac; the website lists supported services. Kinrox Remote is a remote: it does not play video or music on your iPhone.
