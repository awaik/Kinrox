# Kinrox name and logo policy

Effective for releases accompanied by this policy, November 1, 2026.
This policy concerns the Kinrox name, logos, and app icons as indicators of
origin. It is separate from the copyright licenses for code and artwork.
It asserts only rights held by the relevant rightsholder under applicable law;
it does not claim that a trademark has been registered.

## Permitted references

- Identify Kinrox truthfully in documentation, reviews, compatibility statements,
  source attribution, and links to the official project.
- Redistribute an unchanged official GPL Mac release under the Kinrox name,
  including for a fee, provided its origin is accurately stated and no official
  reseller status, sponsorship, or support arrangement is implied.
- Identify a modified version as a fork of Kinrox, while clearly naming its
  maintainer and stating that it is unofficial and modified.

Unmodified remote copies may be shared only as their accompanying license permits.

## Avoiding confusion

Do not describe an unofficial build, download site, service, subscription, or
support offer as official, approved, or operated by the Kinrox maintainer without
permission. Do not use the name or icons as the primary branding of a different
product in a way that is likely to confuse people about its origin.

Use your own product name and icon when marketing a separately branded fork;
keep truthful source attribution such as "Based on Kinrox; independently
maintained by ...". The software licenses' copyright notices must remain intact.

No permission is granted to register confusingly similar product names, domains,
or marks, or to imply that the maintainer provides support for third-party builds.
The official project is https://github.com/awaik/Kinrox and the official website
is https://kinrox.app/.

## Limits

This policy does not restrict rights granted under the GPL to run, modify,
redistribute, or sell the Mac software, and cannot be used to require a commercial
software license. It does not retract rights previously granted for artwork.
Fair use, nominative use, exhaustion, and other rights provided by applicable law
are not restricted. Permissions outside this policy require the rightsholder's
written authorization, requested through the project contact channels.

Verbatim copying and redistribution of this policy are permitted.
