Spotify connection terms

These terms apply when you enable Spotify in Kinrox. Kinrox is maintained by awaik (https://github.com/awaik/Kinrox); support and privacy inquiries: https://github.com/awaik/Kinrox/issues. Kinrox is independent of Spotify and is not endorsed by Spotify. These terms apply upon your acceptance in the app.

Your account and choices

Connecting is optional. Use your own Spotify developer app and an authorized Spotify Premium account. Spotify controls API access and quotas; access may change or stop. Spotify's own terms continue to apply. Audio plays through Spotify Soloist, downloaded directly from Spotify to your Mac at your request. Each user supplies a personal Soloist API key. One-time pairing uses Spotify on iPhone; playback then works with Spotify on Mac and iPhone closed. Soloist handles decoding and its audio cache; Kinrox does not record or export audio. Soloist builds expire after 90 days and require an update.

Library changes

Your actions can save or remove tracks and albums, follow or unfollow artists, create playlists and change playlists you own or can edit. Removing a track from favorites also removes every occurrence of that track from your editable playlists. Selecting an album does not save its tracks individually. Changes take effect in your Spotify account and can be visible in other Spotify clients.

Playback queue

Kinrox keeps the playback queue locally and starts one Soloist process for each queue occurrence. It creates no playback helper playlist. Existing Kinrox Queue playlists remain in Spotify. Their non-secret identifiers are retained locally to exclude them from library changes; you may delete those playlists yourself in Spotify.

Collection and use of data are subject to the privacy policy displayed with these terms. You can also revoke access in your Spotify account's Apps page: https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/.

Privacy Policy — Spotify connection

Version 2026-11-03. The Kinrox maintainer, awaik, is responsible for Kinrox's processing described here. This supplement applies to the optional Spotify integration. The general Kinrox privacy policy is available at https://kinrox.app/privacy/. Contact: https://github.com/awaik/Kinrox/issues. Do not post tokens, personal library contents or other private information in public issues.

Access and purposes

After you accept the terms and authorize Spotify, Kinrox on Mac reads your account identifier and display name, saved tracks and albums, followed artists, accessible playlists and their contents, playback state and available devices. It uses this information to show your library and control Spotify. It can save and remove favorites, follow artists, create playlists and edit owned or collaborative playlists when you use those controls. Removing a favorite also removes its occurrences from editable playlists.

Storage and recipients

Refresh credentials are stored in the Mac Data Protection Keychain. Access tokens stay in memory. Library metadata is cached in a local SQLite database in the Mac app’s Caches directory. The verified account identifier and display name are stored in Mac preferences. The supplied Client ID, helper playlist association and accepted document version are stored in Mac preferences. Kinrox has no server, analytics or telemetry, and the maintainer receives none of this Spotify data. Spotify receives requests directly from the Mac and handles them under its privacy policy: https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/. Spotify sees network information such as the Mac's public IP address. The personal Soloist API key is stored in Keychain. The Connect session is stored in a private account directory; Soloist uses a separate audio cache limited to 200 MB. The key is passed to the signed Soloist process in launch arguments, which other processes under the same user account can read. Soloist's loopback control socket has no authentication or Origin check; local programs or web pages with loopback access can send playback commands.

iPhone and links

The paired iPhone receives track, album, artist and playlist metadata, queue state and playback controls through Kinrox's encrypted local network connection. It never receives Spotify credentials or calls the Spotify Web API. If you tap Open in Spotify, the iPhone or Mac opens the relevant item in Spotify or its website. That handoff can use the internet and is governed by Spotify's privacy policy. Remote metadata is held in memory and cleared on disconnection or a source/account reset.

Disconnect and retention

In Kinrox on Mac, open Settings → Spotify → Disconnect. Kinrox stops its Soloist process and requests, removes the refresh credential, Soloist API key and its own session/audio cache, and clears account data from memory. Non-secret legacy queue playlist identifiers and the downloaded engine remain. It stops automatic reconnection. The acceptance version is an account-free preference. Changes already made in Spotify, including playlists and favorites, remain there. Disconnect does not revoke the authorization at Spotify; remove the app at https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/ to revoke it. You can delete retained helper playlists directly in Spotify. Cached metadata expires after 7 days without revalidation. Disconnect automatically deletes the SQLite cache, its sidecar files and the saved account binding.

Cookies and general Kinrox data

Kinrox adds no analytics or cookies. Its Spotify API sessions use neither persistent cookies nor a disk response cache. Browser login and Spotify websites may use their own cookies; manage those in your browser and Spotify's privacy settings. The general Kinrox policy explains local file bookmarks, pairing credentials, Sparkle updates and GitHub hosting. Spotify is a third-party beneficiary of this policy and the connection terms. Material changes to this supplement will be shown before a new connection is enabled.
