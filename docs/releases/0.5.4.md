# Kinrox 0.5.4

Mac player release for Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 14 or newer.
The companion iPhone remote is not published to the App Store in this release.

## What's New

- **Uninterrupted long album playback.** Soloist occurrence tracking now recognizes earlier final-buffering transitions (`up to 5s` before nominal duration), applies boundary tolerance to `naturalEnd`, and latches terminal completion across `paused`, `idle`, and zero-position reset frames at the end of a track.
- **Race-free track transitions and endpoint startup.** The Soloist process supervisor checks whether the child process is exiting before reporting a WebSocket disconnect, preventing the 200 ms disconnect timer from preempting a clean `exit(0)` track boundary, and retries `lsof` listener verification until the startup deadline.
- **Protected startup, resume reconciliation, and background playback.** Early `playback_changed(status: "playing")` events during initial track load no longer trigger a premature 2-second state-query timeout, and Kinrox holds a macOS activity assertion during active Spotify playback and track transitions so App Nap and idle sleep do not stall album progression.
- **Automatic skipping of restricted tracks and seamless recovery at track end.** Queue advancement skips unplayable (`isPlayable == false`) tracks instead of stopping the album, and pressing Play after an occurrence stops at the end of a track advances to the next playable item (or restarts from `0:00`) without seeking to the final second.

## Included in 0.5.4

- Standalone Spotify playback through separately downloaded **Spotify Soloist**, with `Spotify.app` fully closed, plus local queue management, favorites with undo, artist/album popovers, per-collection sorting, and encrypted LAN iPhone remote controls.
- Local video playback for MP4, MKV, MOV, M4V, AVI, WebM, and TS powered by bundled libmpv 0.41.0, FFmpeg 9.0.1, OpenGL, and VideoToolbox hardware acceleration.
- Audio and subtitle track selection, recursive external subtitle/audio discovery, and per-folder track preferences.
- Matrix screensaver (`Screensaver → Matrix`) and Sparkle 2 update reminders (`Kinrox → Check for Updates…`).
- Shared localization in 30 languages with English fallback.

## Requirements and Notes

- macOS 14.0 or newer on Apple Silicon (arm64).
- Runtime verification for this release was performed on macOS 26.5.2 (Apple Silicon). Minimum-OS metadata was verified for all bundled binaries; a separate macOS 14 machine was not tested.
- To install manually, open `Kinrox-0.5.4-arm64.dmg` and drag `Kinrox.app` to `Applications`. Existing installations of Kinrox 0.3.0 or newer can also update through `Kinrox → Check for Updates…`.
- `Kinrox-0.5.4-dependency-sources.tar.gz` contains the corresponding source archives, build recipes, patches, and license texts for the bundled open-source libraries.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists SHA-256 checksums for the release assets.
