# Kinrox 0.5.11

Mac player release for Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 14 or newer.

## What's New

- **Home & Media Library.** The start screen is now **Home** (`Home | Spotify`), separating media stored on your Mac or external drives from Spotify. Add folders to index movies, TV series and local music in the background, and browse **Overview**, **Movies & Series** with inline series cards and episode strips, **Music on Mac** and **Recent**. Opening or dragging individual video or audio files still works right away without setting up folders.
- **Local music on Mac.** Play `FLAC`, `ALAC`, `MP3`, `M4A`, `AAC`, `WAV`, `AIFF`, `OGG` and `OPUS` files directly on Home with album grids, lossless and sample-rate badges, genre and rating filters, a docked mini-player and a queue popover with shuffle and repeat. Mac media keys and paired iPhones control local music alongside video and Spotify.
- **Continue Watching & offline drives.** Kinrox remembers where you paused a video and advances **Continue Watching** to the next unwatched episode when you finish one. Unplugged external drives keep their indexed catalog, cached artwork and watch progress until you reconnect them.
- **Ratings, genres & artwork with one-click control.** Watched folders look up ratings, genres, episode details and artwork from Cinemeta, TVMaze, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive by default. Requests send clean media titles, album artist names when needed and public catalog IDs over HTTPS without cookies, credentials, file paths or user identifiers. Use **Load ratings, genres, and artwork** in either **Folders** or **Settings** to turn lookups off and cancel active requests. Updated privacy disclosures cover all 30 languages.

## Requirements and Notes

- macOS 14.0 or newer on Apple Silicon (arm64).
- The remote protocol is unchanged: Kinrox Remote 0.2.2 and older remotes (including Lock Screen controls) keep working with this release and control local music on Mac without receiving file paths.
- Standalone Spotify playback continues to use separately downloaded Spotify Soloist, with Spotify.app fully closed. Spotify Premium, your own Client ID, a Soloist API key and one-time phone pairing are required; Soloist is not bundled with Kinrox.
- Runtime verification is limited to macOS 26.5.2 (Apple Silicon). Minimum-OS metadata is checked for bundled binaries; no separate macOS 14 runtime test was performed.
- Automated checks, performance measurements, notarization and mounted-app evidence are recorded in [Mac 0.5.11 Verification](../runbooks/0.5.11-VERIFICATION.md).
- To install manually, open `Kinrox-0.5.11-arm64.dmg` and drag `Kinrox.app` to `Applications`. Existing installations of Kinrox 0.3.0 or newer can also update through `Kinrox → Check for Updates…`.
- `Kinrox-0.5.11-dependency-sources.tar.gz` contains corresponding sources, build recipes, patches and license texts for bundled open-source libraries. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists release-asset checksums.
