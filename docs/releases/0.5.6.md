# Kinrox 0.5.6

Mac player release for Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 14 or newer.
The companion iPhone remote is not published to the App Store in this release.

## What's New

- **Video Home & Recent while a movie is open.** Press `Esc` in windowed mode, click `‹ Home & Recent` in the titlebar or `Video Home` in the playback overlay, or choose `View → Show Video Home` to pause playback and browse `Recent` files without closing the current video. Includes a **Continue Watching** banner (`Back to Video` / `Close Video ⌘⌫`), quick file/remote actions, and compact titlebar navigation at narrow window widths.
- **Long-session memory and CPU optimizations in Spotify & Video modes.** Reused a single `URLSession` across Soloist track launches with immediate task cancellation on teardown and nanosecond pre/post-verified binary stamp caching; unmounted idle OpenGL video surfaces in Spotify mode when no video is loaded; enabled 64 MiB SQLite `mmap` (`WAL`) with streaming batch decoding and 64-bit `FNV-1a` digest diffing; interned `String` and `[MusicArtist]` buffers in `MusicCatalog.merge` while pruning disposable search/discovery entities; and isolated SwiftUI row popover/focus observation and lazy stack boundaries.

## Included in 0.5.6

- Standalone Spotify playback through separately downloaded **Spotify Soloist**, with `Spotify.app` fully closed, plus v3 three-column navigation, persistent compact queue with drag-and-drop expansion, Similar & Discovery engine, favorites with undo, and encrypted LAN iPhone remote controls.
- Local video playback for MP4, MKV, MOV, M4V, AVI, WebM, and TS powered by bundled libmpv 0.41.0, FFmpeg 9.0.1, OpenGL, and VideoToolbox hardware acceleration.
- Audio and subtitle track selection, recursive external subtitle/audio discovery, and per-folder track preferences.
- Matrix screensaver (`Screensaver → Matrix`) and Sparkle 2 update reminders (`Kinrox → Check for Updates…`).
- Shared localization in 30 languages with English fallback.

## Requirements and Notes

- macOS 14.0 or newer on Apple Silicon (arm64).
- Runtime verification for this release was performed on macOS 26.5.2 (Apple Silicon). Minimum-OS metadata was verified for all bundled binaries; a separate macOS 14 machine was not tested.
- To install manually, open `Kinrox-0.5.6-arm64.dmg` and drag `Kinrox.app` to `Applications`. Existing installations of Kinrox 0.3.0 or newer can also update through `Kinrox → Check for Updates…`.
- `Kinrox-0.5.6-dependency-sources.tar.gz` contains the corresponding source archives, build recipes, patches, and license texts for the bundled open-source libraries.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists SHA-256 checksums for the release assets.
