Kinrox 0.1.0 for Mac

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Drag Kinrox.app to Applications, then open it from Applications.
Open a local video with Command-O or drag the video into the player.
Homebrew, Xcode, and a separate mpv installation are not required.

The application is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.
The companion iPhone remote is available as source; App Store publication
is planned separately. The Mac player works without the remote.

Source, licenses, release notes, and issues:
