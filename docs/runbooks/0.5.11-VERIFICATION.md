# Mac 0.5.11 Verification

Release authorized by the owner on 2026-11-19, invoked through `general-release`
(`/general-release`). Mac version 0.5.11, build 21, Apple Silicon, minimum deployment
target macOS 14.0. Runtime checks ran on macOS 26.5.2 (25F84); no separate macOS 14
runtime test was performed. Kinrox Remote is unchanged (0.2.2, build 4).

## Contents

- Unified Home & Local Media Library (`17 · Home — Media Library`, Figma `319:304`):
  replaces `WelcomeView` with `HomeWorkspaceView` (`Home | Spotify`), `Overview`
  (`Continue Watching`, `Movies & Series`, `Music on This Mac`), `Movies & Series` with
  expandable `SeriesDetailCard` and episode strip, `Music on Mac` with album grid and
  `LocalTrackTable`, and `Recent`, while keeping ad-hoc single-file video and local audio
  open/drop without folder setup.
- Watched folders & SQLite media library (`media-library.sqlite`): recursive background
  scanning (`FSEvents` + volume mount/unmount/wake notifications), incremental
  `mtime + size` updates, local audio tag/cover extraction (`FLAC`, `Ogg/Opus`, `MP3`,
  `M4A/ALAC/AAC`, `WAV`, `AIFF`), video watch-progress persistence with next-episode
  `Continue Watching`, and offline external-drive retention (`is_offline`).
- Headless local audio playback (`MPVAudioEngine` inside `MPVKit/` behind
  `LocalAudioEngine` + `LocalAudioViewModel`): 20-second paused engine parking with saved
  position and volume restoration, docked `LocalAudioMiniPlayerBar` + queue popover,
  3-way `PlaybackCoordinator` serialization (`.video`, `.localAudio`, `.music`), Mac Now
  Playing artwork/commands, and LAN `RemoteServer` support using existing `RemoteProtocol`
  v1 without transmitting filesystem paths.
- Keyless open metadata & artwork enrichment: default-on HTTPS lookups to Cinemeta,
  TVMaze (CC-BY-SA attributed in UI and `NOTICE`), MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive
  (`archive.org`), controlled by the shared `"Load ratings, genres, and artwork"` toggle
  in the Folders sheet and Settings, plus updated privacy policy, Privacy card and FAQ
  disclosures across all 30 website locales (dated `2026-11-19`).
- Runner model defaults in `.agents/skills/{general-release,research-execute}` updated per
  the 2026-11-18 Codex token-economy decision (`gpt-6.2-sol` / `high`).
- Review fixes (agy review, Codex apply, orchestrator control): Cover Art Archive two-hop
  HTTPS redirect (`coverartarchive.org` -> `archive.org` -> `*.archive.org`) with 8 MiB
  index cap and transient-error retry preservation; Matrix screensaver guards for local
  audio and `Home` / `⌘1` navigation; post-completion watch-progress checkpointing on
  backward seek or EOF replay; independent video (`8.0+`) and audio (`4.0+`) rating
  filters with global-only `Overview` filtering; remounted ancestor folder coalescing and
  partial-scan availability recovery; ad-hoc audio tag size/queue-label refresh; localized
  local-audio error alerts; and `KINROX_RELEASE_SMOKE_TEST=1` catalog/watcher isolation.

## Performance measurements

Completed by the orchestrator on macOS 26.5.2 (25F84) (`arm64`, `-configuration Release`,
pinned portable `Vendor/iina-1.5.0` libmpv) via `HomeReleaseBenchmarkTests` at a
`1440×940` viewport with Kinrox volume set to `0` (`PlayerViewModel` and
`LocalAudioViewModel`) and Mac system volume untouched:

- Idle memory, CPU & wakeups (4 s steady-state windows): empty Home (`0` watched folders)
  `70.33 MiB` physical footprint (`127.09 MiB` RSS), `0.29%` of one core, `10.66`
  interrupt wakeups/s; populated Home (`241` mixed video & tagged FLAC files + cached
  downsampled artwork) `83.00 MiB` physical footprint (`146.97 MiB` RSS), `0.24%` of one
  core, `11.64` wakeups/s.
- Folder scan latency & `@MainActor` responsiveness (`240 -> 241` mixed files, enrichment
  off): cold initial scan + tag/cover extraction `103.20 ms` (max `@MainActor` 10 ms tick
  `10.56 ms`), warm 0-change rescan `157.30 ms`, warm 1-file delta rescan `50.20 ms`.
- Headless local audio (`MPVAudioEngine`, FLAC at `volume = 0`): cold start `207.10 ms`,
  seek `0.25 ms`, `Next` `20.31 ms`, pause `0.10 ms`, warm resume `22.14 ms`,
  post-20s-parking resume `23.53 ms` (restored `position = 5.46 s`, `volume = 0`);
  sustained playback (6 s) `86.33 MiB` physical footprint, `2.98%` CPU, `10.26`
  wakeups/s; short pause (4 s) `86.36 MiB`, `0.56%` CPU, `12.36` wakeups/s; parked idle
  after `>20 s` pause (4 s) `86.05 MiB` (`124.72 MiB` RSS), `0.23%` CPU, `9.90` wakeups/s.
- Cross-source transition at `volume = 0`: `.localAudio -> .video` `30.65 ms`,
  `.video -> Home (Esc)` `36.11 ms`, `Home -> .localAudio` `20.30 ms`, preserved video
  position `0.38 s`, zero double-audio observed.
