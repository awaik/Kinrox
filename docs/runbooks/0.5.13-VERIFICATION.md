# Mac 0.5.13 Verification

Release authorized by the owner on 2026-11-24 (direct release after resolving all 15 review
findings from the prior review round). Mac version 0.5.13, build 23, Apple Silicon, minimum
deployment target macOS 14.0. Runtime checks ran on macOS 26.5.2 (25F84); no separate macOS 14
runtime test was performed. Kinrox Remote is released separately.

## Contents

- **Lazy video engine and OpenGL initialization** (`LazyMPVPlayer`, `VideoView`): defers
  `MPVPlayer` and `CGLContext` creation until a video actually loads, releases the engine on
  `stop()`, `park()` and terminal decode error (`inFlightLoads == 0`), and preserves
  `KINROX_RELEASE_SMOKE_TEST=1` verification via `verifyStartup()`.
- **OpenGL fence leak fix** (`MPVRenderContext`): returns a `@convention(c)` no-op for
  `glFenceSync` (`disabledFenceSync`), eliminating per-frame `GLDFenceRec` accumulation in
  `AppleMetalOpenGLRenderer` during long playback sessions on Apple Silicon.
- **Bounded demuxer caches and Soloist session cleanup** (`MPVPlayer`, `MPVAudioEngine`,
  `SoloistProcessSupervisor`): caps video demuxer caches to `32MiB` / `8MiB` with
  `hwdec-extra-frames=3`, local audio demuxer caches to `8MiB` / `2MiB`, and invalidates and
  clears the `URLSession` when Soloist stops.
- **30-minute auto-parking of hidden paused videos** (`PlayerViewModel+Parking`,
  `PlayerViewModel+Screensaver`, `PlayerWindowController`, `TrackController`, `ControlsBar`,
  `TrackCommands`): releases the video engine only after 30 minutes of pause or EOF while the
  video surface is hidden (window occluded, Home, Spotify, or Matrix screensaver), preserving
  visible paused/EOF frames on screen. Restores exact position, EOF state, and active audio and
  subtitle tracks (including external SRT files and Off even when folder remembering is off);
  keeps the prior parked session and Home intact on failed opens; ignores cold-start
  `idle-active=true` while loading; routes ControlsBar and TrackCommands through
  `openTrackPanel(kind:)`; and avoids pausing music or losing sub-5 s progress when opening
  tracks from the library.
- **Agent watchdog and monitor hardening** (`.agents/bin/*`, `.agents/lib/*`, `.agents/tests/*`,
  `.agents/skills/*`, `AGENTS.md`): `SIGHUP` and `BaseException` process-group cleanup,
  `step_type = 17` filtering for stream interruptions, writable-only `lsof -Fan` / `fdinfo`
  filtering, explicit fresh/resumed baseline snapshots, `last_active` preservation and periodic
  log-rotation refresh.

## Performance measurements

Measured on macOS 26.5.2 (25F84) (`arm64`, `-configuration Release`) with Kinrox volume `0`
and Mac system volume untouched:

- Cold startup idle physical footprint in Home (lazy `MPVPlayer`, no video opened): reduced
  from ~116–120 MB to ~60 MB (peak ~79 MB, `IOAccelerator` 16 KB).
- OpenGL fence allocation during sustained video playback: flat at 56 fixed `timer_pool_start`
  objects (eliminating the continuous `GLDFenceRec` per-frame growth previously observed at
  123,918 objects).
- Spotify standalone limits remain as recorded in [Soloist Verification](SOLOIST-VERIFICATION.md)
  and were not re-measured.
